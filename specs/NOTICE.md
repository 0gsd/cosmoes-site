# The texts

The specifications in this folder — MacroCOSM OES, Thym, Ascribe, Comprise OES, and OES, the paradigm map — are

    Copyright © 2026 Graham Smith.

These are the canonical texts: the editions published at cosmoes.org and its hosts are built from these files and
from nothing else (`specs.json` names each text's edition and host; `scripts/build_specs.py` builds the pages). A
copy of a text that an application ships or syncs is a copy of an edition published here.
