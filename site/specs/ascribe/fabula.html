Edition 0.5.4 · 2026-11-03. The canonical edition at cosmoes.org. The name is Ascribe, without a number; the edition is a field of the status line, and the declaration stays ascribe/1. Section 13, "What is not built", is withdrawn: the gaps it listed are stated where they fall, as reserved for a later edition (the process that asks the dialectic's questions, section 8; the filter that applies the private marker, section 10; the bridge to Comprise, section 12); Ascribe-Connect, isographs on an index and consented match tokens stay named in section 12 as what the projections are for. Section 14, "Decisions in this version", becomes this appendix. The reference implementation is named, not pathed; MacroCOSM OES and Thym are cited at their cosmoes.org homes; where the text deferred to the code, it is normative. No rule of the format, no check and no example changed.
