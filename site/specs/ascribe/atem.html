Glossary

a company (Comprise)
a company (Comprise), called holotype in the files — defined in How to read this
a person (Ascribe)
a person (Ascribe), called holograph in the files — defined in How to read this
candidate
A candidate is an ordinary node of its kind — defined in §9 Reading a person's own texts
cryptograph
A cryptograph is the holder's encrypted share: a Thym package in its encrypted form — defined in §14.2 The cryptograph
dialectic
The dialectic is the core's one way of asking a person what something means… — defined in §8 The dialectic, applied
holder
The holder of a holograph is its subject, who keeps it — defined in How to read this
holomorph
A holomorph is the holder's morph — defined in §14.5 Morphs
ingest
another volume, a library or an index copies the holograph, or part of it, in — defined in §11 The private filter
isograph
An isograph (ascribe-isograph/0) is a person's public projection — defined in §14.1 The isograph
isomorph
An isomorph is a holomorph the holder chose to share — defined in §14.5 Morphs
match token
A match token lets a search find a cryptograph without reading it — defined in §14.3 Match tokens
package
a Thym package carries it, sealed or not, a cryptograph included (Thym, its section 15) — defined in §11 The private filter
projection
a program projects it: an isograph, match tokens, an isomorph (section 14) — defined in §11 The private filter
release
A release is one act of the holder's by which something left the holograph — defined in §13 Releases
transfer
the holograph, or part of it, is copied off the holder's machine, a backup included — defined in §11 The private filter
