The OES specifications

An OES, an organizational enumeration system, describes a domain by enumerating everything in it

Glossary

built-in
a kind of volume the core defines, declared by a manifest every reader ships with — defined in §2 Scales
Checks
constraints on the whole volume, run on every write — defined in §4 What MacroCOSM gives every MiniCOSM
COSM
the family's name, from kosmos, order and world; it is not an acronym — defined in §2 Scales
Densities
one meaning at three densities: the fabula, a short told from it, and its atem — defined in §4 What MacroCOSM gives every MiniCOSM
edition
a published state of one of these texts — defined in §9 Editions
Items, fields and references
a row with a stable id and a kind; its fields are columns — defined in §4 What MacroCOSM gives every MiniCOSM
MacroCOSM
an OES: the shared grammar, and its core, what every reader has built in — defined in §2 Scales
MicroCOSM
one organization's extension on top of a MiniCOSM — defined in §2 Scales
MiniCOSM
a domain's OES with a specification of its own, declared by a manifest (section 5) — defined in §2 Scales
Open questions
a value nobody has given yet — defined in §4 What MacroCOSM gives every MiniCOSM
Paths, and a URI form
every item and field has an address that respells its id as a location, mozilla-foundation/P/offering/creative-futures — defined in §4 What MacroCOSM gives every MiniCOSM
Renderers
anything that turns a volume into another form — defined in §4 What MacroCOSM gives every MiniCOSM
Saved and signed versions
an immutable copy of the files at a moment, with a manifest of hashes — defined in §4 What MacroCOSM gives every MiniCOSM
The atem
The atem, one line — defined in §3 The model: decompile, fabula, compile, short, atem
The dialectic
questions put to a person about what a source means, and the answers — defined in §4 What MacroCOSM gives every MiniCOSM
The envelope
the columns every item has: id, kind, name, summary, status, visibility, provenance — defined in §4 What MacroCOSM gives every MiniCOSM
The fabula

The input
The input, a text — defined in §3 The model: decompile, fabula, compile, short, atem
The manifest
the machine-readable declaration of a MiniCOSM or a built-in, one .cosm file — defined in §4 What MacroCOSM gives every MiniCOSM
The private/public split
two formats, not one with a setting: the complete private description — defined in §4 What MacroCOSM gives every MiniCOSM
The short
The short, a telling — defined in §3 The model: decompile, fabula, compile, short, atem
